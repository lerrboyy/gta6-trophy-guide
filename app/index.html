Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Story — play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission (trophies 2–14, 22–27).
  2. Side — stranger strands, activities and partner missions (trophies 15, 16, 19–21, 25, 29, 33–34, 36–41, 44–51).
  3. Collectibles — shooting range, drone races, meditation spots, cards, WAiNK, vehicles, areas, gangs, garages (trophies 17, 18, 28, 30, 31, 35, 42, 43, 52, 54, 55).
  4. Cleanup — 50 challenges, $100,000 spent, 100% completion, then the platinum (trophies 32, 53, 14, 1).

Missable trophies

8 trophies can be missed if you pass the moment: Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Gunslinger, Sneakthief, Capital Offense, Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess. Do them when the chance appears, or keep a save before each.
